Profunda & Glitter Bloc — Backstory

LORE SUMMARY FOR GM REFERENCE · RIMWARD P.74 · EP2E P.190
PROFUNDA
Largest settlement on Enceladus. 850,000 residents. Began as an automated mining station; built out rapidly during the Fall by Indonesian and New Zealand refugees. The dominant cultural character is Southeast Asian Muslim — the habitat once boasted the tallest minarets in the Saturnian system. Every citizen holds at least one share in Profunda Corp, which mines and refines organic chemicals from Enceladus's subsurface. Dividends cover basic necessities. The rich are very rich, but no one here is poor.
ENCELADUS
Saturn's sixth-largest moon. Geologically active — famous for cryovolcanic plumes and water-ice geysers venting into space. Gravity: 0.011g surface (the venue interior is at a slightly higher effective 0.113g due to habitat engineering). Atmosphere dangerously toxic outside. Subsurface water-filled caverns extend 80km or more down — the beehive tunnels run through ice-rock above these.
GOVERNANCE
Extropian — no laws, only contracts. The local Extropian judiciary is on retainer for disputes. Profunda Corp is not a government; it is a corporation whose shareholders happen to constitute the population. All business, including event attendance, is a contractual relationship. There is no outside police jurisdiction inside a private venue.
GLITTER BLOC
Profunda's homegrown luxury morph design house. Undisputed leader in high-fashion sleeves system-wide — outclasses Skinaesthesia and Skinthetic in the prestige tier. Provides morphs to the most prominent metacelebrities and socialites across the solar system. Hits include multiple Sylph variants, the Saturn Elite, the ring flier, and numerous chimeric designer creature lines. They own the venue outright. Their security is the law inside it.
BEEHIVE TYPE
Profunda is a classic beehive hab — tunnels, caverns, and excavated spaces in microgravity moon-rock. The event venue occupies a purpose-excavated ballroom cavern with adjacent carved side-chambers. Walls are dressed Enceladus ice-rock behind holo-paneling and bioluminescent fixtures. The largest surface domes (50km+ across) are where the wealthiest residents live; the venue is subsurface, which in Profunda means opulent rather than austere.
◈ 0.113g — Low Gravity Effects on Play
  • Champagne pours in lazy arcs and hangs in the air. Spilled drinks drift. Aerial glow-puffers float naturally through the space.
  • Guests move with exaggerated elegance — or barely contained chaos if their morph is gravity-optimised for higher-g environments.
  • A hard shove sends someone sailing 3–5 meters before they can arrest momentum. Combat in the ballroom becomes three-dimensional quickly.
  • Morph check (SOM×2) for anyone unaccustomed to low-g trying to sprint, dodge, or grapple in formal attire.
  • Glitter Bloc's house morphs are all tuned for Profunda gravity. Guests in off-world sleeves may be at a subtle social and physical disadvantage.
  • The VR pods are sealed and gravity-neutral — you float inside them, which is part of the designed experience.

◈ Art on These Walls — Main Ballroom
Borobudur Karmavibhangga Relief Panels (Hutton / History of Asian Art) — Spanning the north wall, backlit in amber. The 9th-century Javanese bas-relief narrative of karma and consequence across 160 panels. An AR overlay has been added: the carved figures now wear contemporary morph designs. The joke is credited to Glitter Bloc's creative director.

Majapahit Gold Repoussé (Hutton) — Two reproduction panels, east and west of the main arch. The great Javanese Hindu empire's goldsmithing; dancing figures surrounded by flame. Signals Profunda's Indonesian heritage to anyone who recognises the iconography.
◈ Read Aloud
The ballroom opens before you like the inside of a carved moon — which is exactly what it is. Vault ribs of dressed ice-rock rise twenty meters overhead, threaded with bioluminescent strips that pulse in slow amber-blue cycles. Aerial plants drift near the ceiling: globular, translucent, each one a hydrogen-filled bag trailing gossamer roots. The floor is forty meters away in any direction and, in this gravity, entirely optional. Several guests have given up on it entirely and are conducting conversations two meters off the ground, champagne floating in lazy spheres beside them.

VR Pod Alcove

⚠ RIFF-RAFFThe pod session manager was hacked 3 days ago. Riff-Raff can pull any user mid-session into a private VR meeting room rather than the Glitter Bloc demo environment. The hack is undetected. Alciato's session will be intercepted. Infosec scan of the pods (DV 25) detects the intrusion.
TacticalA PC in a pod is physically unconscious and vulnerable for 8 minutes. Two pods can be used simultaneously for a shared VR session — the PCs could enter with Alciato if they move fast enough before Riff-Raff intercepts.
◈ Art — VR Pod Alcove
Bubblegum Crisis Design Sheets (BGC Art Book — Knight Sabers Analytics) — Framed concept art on every wall, presented as Glitter Bloc's own creative process: hard-suit schematics, anatomical overlays showing subdermal layers beneath perfect skin, character design sheets annotated in Japanese and English. In-universe these read as early design sketches for the morph lines guests are about to inhabit. The line between fashion illustration and medical diagram has been deliberately erased.
◈ Read Aloud
The walls of the pod alcove display what appear to be design schematics — elegant figures in various poses, annotated with measurement marks and anatomical notes in Japanese and English. They look like fashion sketches. Then you notice the subdermal layer diagrams visible through the illustrated skin, the neural interface mapping where the skull meets the neckline. The pods themselves are white and seamless, humming faintly. An attendant smiles and asks if you'd like to try the Saturn Elite. It only takes eight minutes.
Observation Terrace
≈ 15m × 8m (10sq × 6sq) · floor sits ~22m above ballroom floor · ceiling 4m (3sq) above terrace floor · pressurised dome viewport · Saturn visible · upper level via lift-shaft (3 sec, 0.113g)
CONNECTIONS← Main Ballroom via single lift shaft (3 sec, low-g) · No other access — lift is the only route up or down
AccessUpper level via a single low-g lift shaft (3 seconds). Semi-private — less traffic than the main floor. Status flex: being invited up here signals you matter.
ViewThrough 4cm of transparent composite: Enceladus surface above, cryovolcanic plumes venting ice crystals into the void. Saturn and its rings visible on the horizon depending on orbital position. The sky is genuinely beautiful and genuinely lethal.
NPCs HereGrix (melancholy, watching the plumes). Uthup (delivering a monologue to a trapped guest). Alciato (occasionally steps up for privacy).
Tactical22m above the ballroom floor — but at 0.113g a fall is survivable. Terminal velocity from this height in Enceladus gravity is roughly 7m/s; treat as a 3m fall in standard gravity for damage purposes (1d10 DV, no wound unless critical). A shove off the open terrace edge during a scuffle would be dramatic but not necessarily lethal — more likely to strand someone dangling from a decorative fitting while the lift is on the wrong floor. The lift shaft itself is the only way back up — and it can be called away.

Zen Enso Brushwork (Sherman Lee) — Three small ink circles, each by a different hand. Minimal. Contemplative. Good contrast to the maximalist party below.
◈ Read Aloud
A Song Dynasty landscape scroll hangs opposite the viewport — ink mountains dissolving into mist, a scholar so small you have to look for him, standing on a bridge that might be a brushstroke. Outside, Enceladus is venting. A cryovolcanic plume climbs two hundred kilometres into the black, catching Saturn's light into a column of ice-crystal fire. The scholar, as noted, is not concerned.

Staff Corridors

INFILTRATION REFERENCE
WIDTH
1.8–2.5m wide (2sq) · ceiling 2.4m (2sq) · rough-finished ice-rock, no decoration · utilitarian lighting strips at floor level only
ACCESS
Four concealed staff access points in the main ballroom (behind holo-panels, Perceive DV 20 to spot). Unlocked from the staff side; keycard required from the guest side (Electronics DV 15 to bypass).
LAYOUT
A service ring connecting all major rooms behind the scenes. Connects: kitchen → refresher suite service access → morph staging → security hub → VR pod maintenance hatch → loading dock exit. Running the full circuit takes ~3 minutes at a brisk low-g lope.
PATROLS
One guard walks the staff corridor circuit on a 12-minute schedule. Two stationary drones at the loading dock and the security hub junction. The guard's mesh ID is logged on the venue system — spoofing it (Infosec DV 25) removes the patrol blip from the security feed.
