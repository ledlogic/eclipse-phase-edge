◈ HOW TO READ THESE BLOCKS
Aptitudes (COG/INT/REF/SAV/SOM/WIL) are rated 5–30 for most transhumans. Skills = Aptitude × 2 base + points spent; max 80 in chargen, no hard cap for NPCs. Pools (Insight/Moxie/Vigor/Flex) refresh on recharge. DUR/WT/DR: Durability / Wound Threshold / Death Rating. INIT = (REF + INT) ÷ 5. All stats are GM estimates suited to narrative role — not min-maxed. Wing stats as needed per the scenario's social register.
