◈ THE SPOKES-AVATAR — GM NOTES
FORM
Not a person. A brand voice — tall, fluid, shifting slightly at the edges as if rendered at fractionally slower than real time. The face is beautiful the way a logo is beautiful. Manifests simultaneously across every AR feed in the room and as a physical hologram at the ballroom's center for guests without lenses. The voice arrives from everywhere at once.
VOICE
Never uses "I" — always "we." Warm but not personal. Precise. The kind of voice that has been tuned by committee to trigger no negative associations in any cultural context. Reads as Profundan — slight Southeast Asian cadence in the vowels, not enough to be specific.
DURATION
Approximately four minutes. Guests are still arriving, taking drinks, finding positions. The speech is designed to be half-heard at the edges and fully heard at the center. It does not demand attention — it rewards it.
LIVE STREAM
Every word is broadcast to the mesh. Any PC attempting mesh surveillance, recording, or eavesdropping during the speech will capture it cleanly. This is intentional — Glitter Bloc wants this speech indexed and replayed.
RECON WINDOW
All major NPCs are stationary and visible during the speech. Crowd faces forward. This is the best uninterrupted reconnaissance window in the session. Njál should be scanning the mesh layer. Zahiri should be mapping guard positions. Chi and Hex should be reading every NPC face. Remind the players of this before you begin reading.
PACING
Read slowly. The avatar pauses. It is not nervous — pauses are deliberate brand beats. If you rush it, it sounds like an announcement. If you pace it, it sounds like a philosophy. Aim for the latter.
◈ READ-ALOUD — OPENING ADDRESS

"Welcome.

"You are standing inside a moon.

"Beneath you: approximately sixty kilometres of ice-rock, and below that, an ocean no sunlight has ever reached. Above you — well. You know what's above you. You've seen the plumes on approach. Enceladus is the most geologically alive body in the Saturnian system, and we chose to build here because we believe the same thing it does: that what's inside is worth pushing out into the void.

"We are Glitter Bloc.

"We have been asked, in the past, what it is we actually make. The question usually comes from people who think the answer is morphs. We understand the confusion. The morphs are extraordinary — you'll see the new lines in a moment, and I promise you, the Saturn Elite alone will make at least four of you reconsider your current sleeve before the evening is out.

"But morphs are not what we make.

"We make the gap between who you are and what you could be — smaller.

"Every being in this room tonight is living proof that transhumanity survived the worst thing that has ever happened to it. You are here. You are embodied — or not, as suits you — on your own terms, in this extraordinary subsurface ballroom, drinking things that float slightly in the gravity, surrounded by creatures that exist because someone here thought it would be beautiful if they did.

"That is what we make. We make the because someone thought it would be beautiful possible.

"Profunda has no laws. What it has is contracts — voluntary, enforced, fair. Glitter Bloc operates on the same principle. We offer you a body. You offer us a decision. No coercion. No manufacture of need. We simply show you what exists, and trust you to know yourself well enough to know if you want it.

"Tonight you will see eight new designs. Four biomorphs, two uplifts, one synthmorph that our creative director describes as a controlled argument with gravity, and one thing that does not yet have a category. You will meet them in the flesh. You will, if you choose, inhabit them — the VR pods are open from Phase Four onward, and the experience has been calibrated to be as accurate as we can legally make it without the suite being classified as a resleeving facility.

"There will be music. There will be creatures released into this room at a moment of our choosing. The taiko drummers are warming up. Diva Von Teases will perform later, and we are contractually prevented from telling you more than that.

"You are all, without exception, exactly the kind of people we made this evening for: curious, present, and unwilling to be told what a body is for.

"Profunda is glad you are here. We are glad you are here.

"Enjoy what's possible."

◈ STAGE DIRECTION — END OF SPEECH
The avatar holds for exactly two seconds — long enough to feel like a breath — then dissolves outward from the center, scattering into light that resolves into the first notes of the harmonic overtone chorus. The chorus performers — twelve figures with surgically enhanced larynxes — are already positioned in a ring around the upper tier. The sound arrives before anyone notices them moving.
◈ LINE NOTES — WHAT EACH BEAT DOES
"inside a moon"
Grounds the players in the physical reality of the setting. The subsurface ocean below is canonical Enceladus lore — delivers atmosphere and foreshadows how isolated this venue is. No station police. No outside jurisdiction. They are deep inside a moon.
"what we actually make"
The philosophical pivot. Establishes Glitter Bloc's self-image: not a morph company but an identity company. This framing matters because it's also the frame the PCs are operating inside — identity is the session's core currency.
"gap between who you are"
Lands differently for every PC at the table. Chi is an async hiding what they can do. Killjoy is operating under a cover identity. Njál is a Titanian at a Consortium-adjacent event. The speech is accidentally about all of them.
"worst thing that happened"
The Fall. Never named. The avatar doesn't need to name it. A room full of transhumans who survived knows exactly what this means. It's a moment of genuine shared weight — let it sit.
"no laws, only contracts"
The Extropian principle stated explicitly. Reminds every player that Glitter Bloc security is the only law in the room — and that law is a contract, which means it can theoretically be renegotiated or voided. Killjoy should hear this as an opportunity.
"one thing without a category"
Deliberate hook. Players will assume it's a morph reveal and spend time speculating. It isn't introduced until the runway presentation. Let them wonder — it keeps the players invested in the timeline rather than treating the party as backdrop.
"controlled argument with gravity"
The synthmorph line. In-universe this is something a Glitter Bloc creative director would actually say. If players ask a staffer about it later, the answer is: a new synthmorph frame that exploits 0.113g in ways impossible at higher gravity — built for Profunda specifically. It is not a combat morph. It is beautiful.
"legally make it"
A small admission that the VR pod experience pushes legal limits. In Profunda, "legal" means "within contract." This line is Glitter Bloc protecting themselves from future liability. It also tells the players the pods are immersive enough to matter — which matters when Riff-Raff's hack becomes relevant in Phase 4.
"unwilling to be told what a body is for"
The closing line's real target is the Jovian delegation. Bioconservatives in the room will find this line offensive. Watch Contarato's face. Watch Halsema's face — she has grown to despise bioconservatism and this line will resonate with her differently than with her colleagues.
◈ NPC REACTIONS — DURING & AFTER
Deliver these as observable details during or immediately after the speech — rewards players who are watching the room rather than listening to the avatar. Each is a character tell available to anyone making a Perceive or Kinesics test (DV 10 — easy, the speech lasts four minutes).
ALCIATO
Applauds. Genuinely. Has heard variations of this speech before and still finds it effective — they respect the craft. They also spend approximately thirty seconds during the speech looking at their own hands, which is something Kyo notices and the PCs might.
KYO
Does not applaud. Scans the room during the speech rather than watching the avatar. She is counting the Glitter Bloc drones, mapping the exits, and tracking which guests are watching Alciato rather than the presentation. She notices the PCs noticing her within thirty seconds of the speech ending.
VOX
Is on her third drink. Says audibly, to no one in particular: "Same speech as last year. Better shoes." Then laughs at her own joke. She is the only person in the room who is visibly bored by the avatar, which tells the players she has been here before and knows how this works.
GRIX
Listens attentively to "worst thing that ever happened to it" and then looks at her drink for the rest of that paragraph. PTSD, managed, but the Fall rhetoric lands on her differently than on most. She applauds at the end — correct, precise, three seconds then stops.
CONTARATO
Visibly stiffens at "unwilling to be told what a body is for." Doesn't applaud. His aide leans over and says something quietly — Contarato nods once, face neutral. He is professionally managing his reaction to a room full of people who disagree with his government's foundational position.
HALSEMA
The "unwilling to be told what a body is for" line hits her visibly — she almost smiles, catches herself, looks at Contarato, looks away. Anyone watching her face at that moment sees someone who no longer belongs in the delegation she's standing with. This is the earliest tell of her defection.
UTHUP
Applauds with conviction — the speech's transhumanist framing aligns with Ultimate ideology even if Glitter Bloc's market positioning doesn't. She then immediately turns to the nearest person and begins explaining why the Glitter Bloc model is philosophically correct but commercially inefficient. That person begins looking for an exit.
WADADA
Is not watching the avatar. Is watching Alciato watching the avatar. He is reading the ambassador's body language — assessing mood, timing the approach for the private meeting later. He has done this before. He will do it again.
◈ IMMEDIATELY AFTER — TRANSITION TO MINGLING
The chorus performs for approximately eight minutes — long enough for guests to resettle, refresh drinks, and begin moving. This is the first structured mingling window. The PCs have been introduced by Vox before the speech begins; after it ends she reappears at their elbow.
VOX
"Right. Now the part where everyone pretends they don't know what anyone else is here for. I'll introduce you to Grix first — she's Titanian, she'll be useful, and she's the only person in the room I genuinely like. Come on."
NJÁL — MESH
During the chorus, Njál gets a first clear look at the venue mesh traffic. The anomalous process in the VR pod session manager is visible at this point — a Research or Infosec test (DV 20) spots it as non-Glitter-Bloc code. The timestamp reads three days ago.
RIFF-RAFF
Sends Alciato the first encrypted mesh ping during the chorus — quiet, easy to miss. A Perceive (mesh) test from any PC in the right position (DV 25) catches the traffic signature. It doesn't decrypt — but it establishes that someone is already in contact with Alciato through channels that aren't on the guest list.
