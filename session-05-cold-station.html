If the team can surface this to either party — Cognite learning that an Ozma closure team is running an operation on a station where Cognite has active assets, or Ozma learning that Cognite's Kuiper coverup is connected to the same signal they're trying to suppress — the resulting institutional friction could create space for the team to move. Neither side would welcome the other's presence. That conflict is a resource if the team is smart enough to use it and fast enough to do it before leaving Vesta.
