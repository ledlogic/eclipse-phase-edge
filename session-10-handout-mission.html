◈ SESSION 10 — PLAYER HANDOUT — ECLIPSE PHASE 2E
ALL THAT GLITTERS
GLITTER BLOC SOIRÉE · PROFUNDA · ENCELADUS · SATURN SYSTEM
PRIMARY
Extract the encrypted data module from Danzan Alciato's headware implant by any means available — social, technical, physical, or pharmaceutical. Do not create an incident that makes the news.
SECONDARY
Identify what the Consortium already knows about the outer-system arc. Did Alciato's intelligence service pick up the Gutenberg signal? Do they know about FK7? About the Lithohex? The answers change how much time the team has before hypercorp assets move into the Kuiper.
CAMPAIGN
Kyo has the decryption key for Alciato's data module. She's Oversight — not Consortium. If she gets the key out, it either exposes Alciato or it disappears into another agency's filing system. Deciding who gets the decryption key, and what gets done with the data, is the session's moral question.
◈ SETTING — PROFUNDA, ENCELADUS
LOCATION
Subsurface beehive habitat, Enceladus, Saturnian system. Privately owned by the Glitter Bloc morph-design house. Extravagant ballroom plus numerous side rooms for socialising, private conversations, and freshening up.
GRAVITY
0.113g — less than Luna. Everyone moves with exaggerated elegance or barely contained chaos depending on their morph and experience. Things drift. Drinks float if spilled.
GOVERNANCE
Extropian — no laws, only contracts. Glitter Bloc owns the venue and runs its own security and judiciary on retainer. Guests are contractually bound by Glitter Bloc security dictums on entry. No station police, no outside jurisdiction. Glitter Bloc security is the only law in the room.
ATMOSPHERE
Live-streamed to the mesh. Several hundred guests — figureheads, celebrities, diplomats, and outer-rim personalities. Everyone is dressed to kill. Designer drugs circulating openly. Runway presentation, fork burlesque, dueling DJs.
SECURITY
Glitter Bloc private guards and drones. All diplomats present have small armed personal details. Overt violence triggers immediate Glitter Bloc neutralisation — combatants removed, event continues. The show must go on. Covert action is not their problem unless it becomes visible.
MESH
Open to all guests. VR pods installed for morph demo experiences.
