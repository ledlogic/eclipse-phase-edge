◈ MISSION PREMISE
SOURCE CANON
Core mission: Planetary Consortium ambassador Alciato is attending a private social event. They possess intel on plans to expand Consortium influence in the outer system. The PCs are sent to recover those plans by any means necessary.
SOURCE CANON
Sponsor options: Concerned anarchists/autonomists, Titanian Fleet Intelligence, Extropian hypercorp rivals, or Jovian Space Force Intelligence — whichever works best.
SOURCE CANON
Vox introduction: The sponsor pulls rep strings to get the team on the guest list and hooks them up with Vox Maxine to introduce them and show them around.
ARC ADDITION
Primary objective framing: "Do not create an incident that makes the news" — added to reflect the arc's theme of Firewall operating in the shadows.
ARC ADDITION
Secondary objective: Entirely original. Did Alciato's service pick up the Gutenberg signal? Do they know about FK7, the Lithohex, the Semaphore? None of this is in the source — it connects the nano-op to the arc's ongoing TITAN/Semaphore thread.
ARC ADDITION
Campaign thread (Kyo's decryption key as moral question): The source notes Kyo has the key. The framing of who gets it and what happens to the data as the session's central ethical decision is original — added to give the session an arc-level consequence beyond mission success/failure.
ARC ADDITION
Situation brief opener ("The team returns from the outer system carrying something most of the inner system doesn't know about yet…"): entirely original — positions All That Glitters as the arc's coda, not a standalone political op.
◈ SETTING — PROFUNDA & THE VENUE
SOURCE CANON
Core setting: Extravagant ballroom, subsurface beehive habitat of Profunda, Saturn's moon Enceladus. Gravity 0.113g. Extropian principles — no laws, only contracts. Glitter Bloc owns and provides security. Guests contractually bound to Glitter Bloc security dictums. Numerous side rooms for socialising, private chats, freshening up.
SOURCE CANON
Venue ownership: The event is sponsored by Glitter Bloc, the elite morph-design outfit, which owns the location and provides its own security guards and drones.
EP2E LORE
Profunda backstory: Indonesian and New Zealand refugee roots. Southeast Asian Muslim cultural character. 850,000 residents. Profunda Corp shareholder model — dividends cover basic necessities. Most prosperous non-Titanian habitat in the Saturn system. All from Rimward.
EP2E LORE
Enceladus: Cryovolcanic plumes, subsurface ocean 80km down, ice-rock geology. From EP2e corebook and Rimward.
EP2E LORE
Glitter Bloc: Luxury morph design house, homegrown to Profunda. Sylph variants, Saturn Elite, ring flier. Outclasses Skinaesthesia and Skinthetic in prestige tier. From Rimward.
EXTRAPOLATED
Venue floor plan: The source mentions "numerous side rooms" with no specifics. All individual room identities, dimensions, square counts, connections, tactical notes, and art recommendations are original extrapolations — building what the source implies but doesn't describe.
EXTRAPOLATED
Security detail: Source says Glitter Bloc provides security guards and drones. Guard count (6), drone count (6), patrol circuit (6-min), response times (30 sec / 90 sec), DV ratings, and security hub description are all extrapolated.
ARC ADDITION
Art recommendations (Borobudur panels, Majapahit gold, Utamaro prints, BGC art book schematics in VR suite, Song Dynasty scroll on terrace, Fracture posters backstage): entirely original — drawn from your physical book collection and matched to Profunda's cultural backstory and each room's thematic role.
ARC ADDITION
Low-gravity tactical rules (morph checks for movement, fall damage from terrace, drift mechanics, three-dimensional combat notes): original additions for tactical play.
ARC ADDITION
Staff corridor ring layout, kitchen fab detail, VR pod individual dimensions, observation terrace height and fall math: all original.
◈ EVENT TIMELINE
SOURCE CANON
All six phases exist in the source in sequence: Guests Arrive → Opening Speech → Creature Presentation → Main Runway → VR Pod Exhibits → Fork Burlesque → Dance Party.
SOURCE CANON
Phase labels: Harmonic overtone chorus, six-armed taiko drummers, Diva Von Teases, dueling ring house DJs — all from the source.
EXTRAPOLATED
Phase tactical framing ("ideal reconnaissance window," "COMPLICATION WINDOW," "CRITICAL WINDOW," "MAXIMUM COVER WINDOW"): the source marks phases with square bullets indicating complication opportunities but doesn't name them as tactical windows. The GM-facing framing of each phase as an opportunity type is original.
EXTRAPOLATED
Phase 3 — Alciato/Wadada private negotiation during runway: the source lists this as a complication. The detail that this is the Volkov contract's final terms, and that Halsema approaches Grix in the same window, is drawn from the source but the tactical framing ("both things happen simultaneously — two covert operations the team can observe or interrupt") is original.
ARC ADDITION
Riff-Raff's first mesh ping to Alciato during Phase 1 (detectable with a lucky Perceive roll): original — added to seed the Riff-Raff subplot earlier than Phase 4.
◈ PRINCIPAL NPCS
DANZAN ALCIATO
SOURCE CANON
Morph (Sylph w/Cyberbrain), pronouns (they/them), role (Consortium Diplomatic Service, heads Saturn affairs), personality (manipulative but not ruthless, long view, confident), primary agenda (expand rimward, undermine outer-system powers), secret (Albiorix shipyard plan in encrypted data module headware implant).
EXTRAPOLATED
Full EP2e stat block (aptitudes, skills, pools, DUR/WT/DR, equipment list). Personal security detail count and loadout. The detail that Alciato applauds the opening speech while spending thirty seconds looking at their own hands.
ARC ADDITION
The "outer system intel" section — what Alciato knows about Gutenberg (classified as a brinker habitat disaster), FK7 (unknown), Lithohex (unknown), Semaphore (unknown). The tell that dropping these names causes Alciato to immediately record the conversation. Entirely original — connects this NPC to the arc's TITAN thread.
JOLENA KYO
SOURCE CANON
Morph (Ghost), pronouns (she/her), role (Oversight agent embedded in Alciato's staff, corruption monitoring + protection), personality (vigilant, doting, momma-bear protective), agenda (get evidence of Volkov bribe), secrets (uplift bigotry, holds decryption key).
EXTRAPOLATED
Full stat block. Concealed pistol detail (medium pistol, passed foyer via Oversight credentials — only non-Glitter-Bloc armed person). Surveillance suite app and 11-day comms archive. The detail she notices the PCs noticing her within 30 seconds of the speech ending.
ARC ADDITION
The full moral question framing — hand over key (illegal, career-ending, prevents shipyard) vs refuse (legal, preserves position, shipyard gets built). The source notes she has the key; the decision as the session's central ethical question is original. Also: "She hasn't used it. She's been carrying it for eleven days."
RIFF-RAFF
SOURCE CANON
ID Crew cartel hacker, infomorph only, dry/laconic personality, Titanian fugitive. Hacked VR pods — plans to pull Alciato into a private meeting. If Alciato's offer is insufficient, will sell to Contarato. Knows ID Crew has Contarato's son's ego; Contarato doesn't know son sold himself to the ID Crew for passage.
EXTRAPOLATED
Full infomorph stat block. E-Veil meshware (–30 detection). The detail that the hack is three days old and not on Glitter Bloc's radar. The Infosec DV to detect (25) or co-opt (35) the exploit. The relay node outside the venue as physical location of Riff-Raff's hardware.
ARC ADDITION
The Phase 1 mesh ping to Alciato as an early detectable signal. The possibility of the team entering the VR instance alongside Alciato before Riff-Raff intercepts.
VOX MAXINE
SOURCE CANON
Neo-Avian morph, she/her, Profunda socialite and drug dealer, professional cynic with biting wit, knows everyone. Agenda: be seen, party, increase rep. Secret: knows Wadada has facilitated ego sales to black-market dealers.
EXTRAPOLATED
Full stat block. Specific drug inventory with DVs, doses, and pricing (information, not credits). "Same speech as last year, better shoes." The detail she's on her third drink before the speech ends.
ARC ADDITION
Her role as a Firewall asset (not in the source — source only says the sponsor hooks the team up with her as a social contact). The framing of her as someone who has worked with Firewall before and knows more than she lets on.
GRIX
SOURCE CANON
Titanian Ministry of State, she/they, Olympian morph, abundant nanotats and luminescent eyes. Confident and direct with bitterness/melancholy. Agenda: protect autonomist interests, undermine Jovians and Consortium. Secret: uses substances to manage Fall PTSD; passes intel to anarchist direct-action groups.
EXTRAPOLATED
Full stat block. Specific detail on Klar + Orbital Hash for PTSD management. The staging of her reaction during the opening speech (looks at her drink during the Fall reference). The precision of her applause (three seconds, then stops).
ARC ADDITION
Nothing significant — Grix is used largely as written. The detail that she arranges the Halsema extraction "by the end of the party" (source implies this, we made it explicit).
ALI WADADA
SOURCE CANON
Extropian Fixer, Sylph morph, he/him. Self-obsessed but diligent mediator. Here to meet Alciato, finalise Volkov contract terms, debauchery. Secret: Volkov's real payment to Alciato is intel on Titan's antimatter-production facilities (not just cash).
EXTRAPOLATED
Full stat block. The Volkov intel chip as a physical data chip in his jacket — a second extraction target as valuable as Alciato's module. His rented Glitter Bloc security drone. The detail that he watches Alciato during the speech rather than the avatar.
ARC ADDITION
The framing of the Titan antimatter intel as a potential arc-level find — intercepting Wadada's chip could give the team leverage over both Volkov and Alciato independently of the data module.
CONTARATO, HALSEMA, UTHUP
SOURCE CANON
All three NPCs are entirely from the source — personalities, agendas, secrets, and morphs unchanged. Contarato (Jovian diplomat, Splicer, jovial/deadly serious, limits Consortium influence, secretly considers Consortium a bigger threat than autonomists). Halsema (Jovian staff/defector, Splicer, stolen Jovian intel, sister on Titan, despises bioconservatism). Uthup (Ultimate, Remade, Zamyatin agent, sows division, dry monologues, would be purged if allegiance known).
EXTRAPOLATED
Full stat blocks for all three. Uthup's holdout pistol (second armed person in the room). Halsema's tell during the opening speech — nearly smiling at "unwilling to be told what a body is for." Contarato stiffening at the same line. Uthup applauding with conviction then immediately monologuing at someone.
ARC ADDITION
Contarato's grief as a vulnerability — surfacing the fact that Riff-Raff holds his son's ego is a destabilising move that could shift his agenda mid-session. The source mentions the son situation as Riff-Raff's secret; using it as an active lever on Contarato during play is original.

◈ SUMMARY

The All That Glitters nano-op is two pages. It provides: the mission premise, the setting name and gravity, a six-phase event timeline with bullet-point descriptions, nine NPC entries (each with a morph, personality, agenda, and one secret), six extraction tactics, five complications, and one sentence each for Glitter Bloc security and the open-combat consequence.

Everything that makes this a playable, detailed GM reference document is either extrapolated from that skeleton or added for the Edge of the Light arc specifically.

The arc additions are primarily: the secondary objective (Consortium outer-system intel awareness), the campaign thread (Kyo's key as a moral question), the situation brief connecting this session to the arc's TITAN/Semaphore storyline, Vox as a Firewall asset rather than just a contact, Alciato's specific ignorance of FK7/Lithohex/Semaphore as a tell mechanic, and the entire opening speech.

The EP2e lore additions are primarily: Profunda's cultural and economic backstory, Enceladus geology, and Glitter Bloc's market position — all used to flesh out a setting the source describes in two sentences.
